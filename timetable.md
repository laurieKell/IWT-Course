# Course Timetable

Each day runs 09:00–17:00. Coffee is 15 minutes. Lunch is an hour.

The general order is theory, example based on case studies, application to participant stocks

## Case Studies

Examples based on shark stocks assessed and managed by the IOTC. Participants are encourage to bring their own case studies so that approaches can be appied to stocks of interest.

## Thoughts

- Comparision of CITES/IUCN assessment requirements with those of IOTC.

- PSA/ERA is linked to stock assessment, i.e. an ERA estimates $F_{MSY}$ based on life history characteristics, but does not provide estimates of state relative to reference points. Life histories are also used to develop priors in a stock assessment which can then be used to assess current state, and forecast responses to management.
